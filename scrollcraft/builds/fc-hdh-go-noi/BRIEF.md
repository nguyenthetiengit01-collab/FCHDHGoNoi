# Creative Brief · FC HĐH Gò Nổi

`Self-authored under explicit creative delegation`
User statement: *"Triển khai theo đánh giá tốt nhất của bạn"*

---

## 1. Vibe & References
- **Vibe in four words**: Hào sảng, Gắn kết, Rực lửa, Hoàng gia (Sapphire & Gold).
- **Three references**:
  1. *All or Nothing: Manchester City* (Amazon Prime Sports Documentary) – Real dressing room emotion, relentless passion, tactical brotherhood.
  2. *Moncler Genius / Nike Football Cinematic Campaigns* – Dark royal sapphire atmosphere, high-contrast gold lighting, dimensional typography behind athletes.
  3. *Apple Keynote Athlete Spotlights* – Butter-smooth scroll choreography, restrained motion, focus on human presence and physical depth.

---

## 2. Scroll Journey (Beats)
1. **Act 1: Khí Thế Gò Nổi (Anthem & Identity)** — Multi-plane dimensional hero. The iconic club crest, team portrait in royal blue, atmospheric stadium night mist.
2. **Act 2: 23+ Chiến Binh Đất Quảng (The Squad)** — Horizontal lateral travel through the players. 3D holographic tilt cards displaying player role, hometown hamlet, and battle stats.
3. **Act 3: Rực Lửa Sân 7 Sài Gòn (The Crucible)** — The battle and adrenaline. Sharp high-intensity action shots, split second tackles, goals, and crowd roar.
4. **Act 4: Nghĩa Tình Đồng Hương (The Bond)** — The emotional shift. Post-match warm fellowship, smiles, the shared bowl of mì Quảng, deep roots that travel thousands of kilometers.
5. **Act 5: Chung Một Màu Cờ (The Commitment)** — The Golden Brotherhood Circle. Sponsors, community backing, and the invitation to lace up boots or stand with the team.

---

## 3. Energy Curve
- **Act 1 (Hero)**: *Solemn Pride* (Ground level, deep bass energy, slow anticipation).
- **Act 2 (Squad)**: *Personal Connection* (Steady, observant, human-scale interaction).
- **Act 3 (Tournament - PEAK)**: *Explosive Adrenaline* (Fast visual cuts, high contrast, peak emotional roar).
- **Act 4 (Community)**: *Lyrical Warmth* (Gentle deceleration, heartfelt belonging, quiet breath).
- **Act 5 (Call to Action)**: *Decisive Solidarity* (Pure resolution, shining gold emblem, magnetic focus).

---

## 4. The Feeling Curve & Peak Moment
```
Act 1 · Pride       multi-plane dimensional depth: badge, squad, and stadium mist in deep sapphire
Act 2 · Camaraderie interactive 3D holographic cards displaying each warrior's hometown and stats
Act 3 · Adrenaline  the explosive tournament action: dust, sweat, and the 90th-minute winning celebration [THE PEAK]
Act 4 · Warmth      post-match brotherly embraces, shared laughter, and hometown memories
Act 5 · Resolve     the golden circle of solidarity: one crest, one family, one magnetic pledge
```

### The Peak
- **Sentence a visitor says to a friend**: *"Đó là khoảnh khắc cả đội bóng cùng lao vào ôm nhau ăn mừng bàn thắng quyết định dưới ánh đèn vàng sân 7 rực lửa giữa lòng Sài Gòn."*
- **Lives in**: Act 3 (Rực Lửa Sân 7).

### The Tell-Someone Sentence
- *"It's the site where you don't just see a football team — you feel the heartbeat, dust, and brotherhood of 23 boys from Gò Nổi fighting for their homeland in the heart of Saigon."*

### Authored Silence
- An intentional breath between Act 3 (Tournament Climax) and Act 4 (Fellowship): a silent 0.4vh transitional resting plate with subtle ambient typography so the visitor decompresses before the emotional warmth lands.

---

## 5. Signature Move
- **"Lăng kính Hologram Chiến Binh 3D" (3D Holographic Player Spotlight)**:
  Player cards don't just sit in a carousel. As the visitor scrolls into the squad arena, hovering or touching a card activates an optical chromatic gold gradient reflection that shifts with the cursor/gyroscope angle, separating the player's cutout from their stats card in real 3D parallax.

---

## 6. Visual Aesthetic Family
- **Family**: *Editorial / Modern Cinematic Sport* (Sapphire Blue `#0B1B3D`, Night Pitch `#060D1E`, Champagne Gold `#D4AF37`, Bright Warm Silver `#F8FAFC`).
- Restrained motion, large editorial typography, real authentic player photography (no clay models, no fake vectors).
